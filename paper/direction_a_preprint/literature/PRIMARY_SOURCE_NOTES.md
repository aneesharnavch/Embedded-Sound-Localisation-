# Primary-source completion notes

Checked 8 September 2026 during preprint execution.

- Schroeder (1965), *New Method of Measuring Reverberation Time*, DOI 10.1121/1.1909343: original AIP publisher abstract and metadata read in the browser. Describes response integration yielding ensemble-average decay and exposing non-exponential behavior. Cite for the energy-decay approach, not for the exact band/fitting choices used here.
- DiBiase, Silverman and Brandstein (2001), *Robust Localization in Reverberant Rooms*, DOI 10.1007/978-3-662-04619-7_8: original Springer chapter abstract and bibliographic metadata checked. Full chapter is subscription-only; no access bypassed. Supports broad source-localization/reverberation context. The present frequency-domain steering score is defined explicitly in the paper and independently checked; no uninspected comparative percentage is borrowed.
- Efron (1979), *Bootstrap Methods: Another Look at the Jackknife*, DOI 10.1214/aos/1176344552: original Project Euclid abstract and metadata read. Describes bootstrap sampling for distribution/uncertainty estimation. Our within-scene whole-record resampling is a stated design choice, not a claim that Efron specifically studied acoustic frame dependence.
- Qin et al. (2008): publisher page retrieval did not provide a readable abstract in this run. Retain as metadata-only search lead and omit from the manuscript bibliography.

Crossref metadata were checked against the publisher-visible bibliographic information. The bibliography uses complete author lists rather than et al. Metadata verification alone is not labeled full-text reading. The fixed-scene study is framed as a verification/replication contribution without a first-of-its-kind claim.
