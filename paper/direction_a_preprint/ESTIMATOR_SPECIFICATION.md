# Estimator specification, version 1

The simulation uses three microphones at polar angles 90, 210, and 330 degrees,
with 0.05 m circumradius. Pair spacing is sqrt(3) times that radius. Sound speed
is 343 m/s. Coordinates describe the simulation, not a measured board.

Arrival times t_i give pair delay t_ij = t_i - t_j. With A containing rows
m_i - m_j, the plane-wave relation is A u = -c t. Uniform least squares uses
all three pairs and reports atan2(u_y, u_x), without treating the unconstrained
vector norm as a separate angle correction.

The default frame contains 2048 samples at 48 kHz. Each channel is mean-removed
and multiplied by the same symmetric Hann window. The FFT length is the next
fast length at least twice the frame length. Pair spectra are X_i conj(X_j).
PHAT divides by max(abs(G), 0.001 max(abs(G)) + 1e-12), then retains frequencies
from 300 through 3400 Hz inclusive. The inverse transform is interpolated by
factor 8. A three-point quadratic refinement uses neighboring correlation
values. Candidate peaks and final refined delays obey the physical pair limit
abs(t_ij) <= pair_spacing/c. The final delay is clipped to this interval.

Fractional propagation uses a normalized Kaiser-windowed sinc with beta 8.6
and half-width 32 samples, subject to direct/room convergence checks. The
windowed sinc is a numerical construction, not a new delay-estimation method.
Reference waveform checks evaluate a deterministic sum of harmonic functions
at exact continuous times, independently of the sampled FIR implementation.

All frames, bands, sound speed, geometry, and interpolation settings must be
passed explicitly. Statistical room comparisons use matched signals and noise
under a separately frozen protocol. Historical scores generated by the old
unbounded/non-windowed implementation are not relabeled as scores for this one.
