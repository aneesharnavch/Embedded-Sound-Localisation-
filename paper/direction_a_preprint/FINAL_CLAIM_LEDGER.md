# Final claim ledger: revised v2 campaign

All final manuscript estimates come from the v2 protocol with 1.2-second response support, image extent 76, propagation grid 32 and disjoint v2 input namespaces. The initial campaign is excluded from the final aggregates. Its joint duration/image check failed the numerical target and triggered a complete corrected rerun; see `NUMERICAL_NOTES.md`.

| Claim | Direct evidence | Interpretation and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Final checked numerical discrepancies are below 0.05° | `results/room/revision_verification.json`, `joint_refinement_1p2_vs1p5.json`, `results/sensitivity/run_summary.json` | Independent full-extent spectral error 1.13e-5 and angular error 0.000807°; joint extension max 0.01273°; fixed stochastic subset max 0.02593°. This is a set of passed checks, not a theorem covering every stochastic frame or a physical validation. |
| Arrival rounding increases direct-path error in the paired study | `results/analysis/attribution_variants.csv`, `simple_paired_contrasts.csv` | At 10 dB, RMSE 0.923° versus 2.300°; paired MSE increase 4.438 deg² [4.380, 4.493]. Conditional on the 72 scenes, source, band and estimator. It does not establish an identical rounding penalty in every reflected field. |
| Source startup can create a large apparent accuracy benefit | `onset_trajectory.csv`, `simple_paired_contrasts.csv`, `factorial_contrasts.csv` | High-subset first-frame RMSE 4.157° at onset versus 42.508° with history. The recordings approach the same matched steady state. This changes the observed acoustic state, not the estimator algorithm. The high subset includes both absorption and placement changes relative to the low subset. |
| Rounding and reflected-field effects are not independent additive error magnitudes | `factorial_contrasts.csv` | Positive rounding-by-reflection and negative onset-by-reflection squared-error interactions. No RMSE subtraction is used to create an error budget. Signed wrapped means are secondary descriptive values. |
| Averaging reduces held-out error but leaves scene-dependent residuals at finite duration | `averaging_scene_summary.csv`, `averaging_global_summary.csv`, `prediction_eligibility.csv` | 128-frame held-out RMSE is 0.835° in the low subset and 10.158° in the high subset. Forty-nine scenes pass the training-only concentration rule. These finite records neither establish a universal RT law nor prove an infinite-time floor. Prediction curves omit parameter uncertainty and are approximate. |
| A conservative geometry-only arrival precision bound can account for pair dependence | `PRECISION_CRITERION.md`, `results/precision/verification_summary.json`, `criterion_trials.csv` | The arrival-error box is propagated through -c A⁺B. All 34,560 rounding cases and 12,000 independent perturbations satisfy the exact vertex bound. The bound excludes noisy waveform estimation, reflected paths and peak switching; it is not a hardware sampling-rate requirement. |
| Generalization and method ranking are limited | `sensitivity_summary.csv`, `baseline_paired_contrast.csv`, complete sensitivity frame files | Source-band/geometry/wall changes alter outcomes. Matched SRP has higher RMSE but lower 5° exceedance rate in the chosen subset. No general superiority, optimized geometry or real-device result is claimed. |

The bootstrap resamples five whole independent records within each fixed scene, preserves pairing, and uses 2,000 draws. Narrow pooled intervals are not evidence of representativeness over rooms. Per-scene ranges, complete frame results, failure flags and difficult outcomes are retained. Novelty is framed as a verified attribution/replication contribution; the established estimator, image method, fractional-delay techniques, triangular layout and bootstrap are credited.
