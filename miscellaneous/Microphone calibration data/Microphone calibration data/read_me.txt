This specific folder contains all files pertaining to calibrating the custom built microphone used in the Soni-Sense project , in our particular , the distance based sample's folder contains files of the microphones recording's while having waves of different waveforms played at different frequency's from varying distances and the waveform based folder displays the values pertaining to different waveforms recorded from the microphone while being played at differing distances .


NOTE : the file naming scheme is as follow's 
	'x'_wave_'y'k_'z'
	in this case 'x' refers to the type of wave form (sine, square and triangle) , 'y' refers to the frequency at which the waveform was being played at and 'z' refers to the distance in centimeters at which the speaker was playing 	the afore mentioned waveforms at 100% volume ( as all recording where taken at the same speaker settings , the possibility of bias is near negligible and therefore can be ignored.
